1. Privacy Policy (POPIA)

Ear Clean respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Information we collect

Your name, contact details, preferred language and platform, the reason for your consultation, and health information you share with us during the consultation.

Why we collect it

To schedule and conduct your consultation, provide professional advice and referral letters, process payment, meet legal and professional record-keeping duties, and communicate with you about your appointment.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers needed to deliver our service (such as video, payment and email providers), with professionals you are referred to when you ask us to, or when the law requires.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. Clinical records are kept for the period required by the Health Professions Council of South Africa and applicable law, then securely destroyed.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to processing, or withdraw consent, subject to legal retention duties. Contact us using the details on our website. You may also complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

2. Terms & Conditions

By booking with Ear Clean you agree to these terms.

  • Service: a 10-minute virtual audiology consultation for R200. Time beyond 10 minutes may not be available.
  • Bookings: a booking is confirmed once payment is received and we have confirmed your time.
  • Your responsibilities: give accurate information, join on time, and ensure a quiet place and a working connection or phone line.
  • Late arrival: the consultation still ends at its scheduled time.
  • Conduct: abusive behaviour may lead to the consultation ending without refund.
  • Recording: consultations are not recorded without your explicit consent.
  • Liability: to the extent allowed by law, Ear Clean is not liable for losses arising from reliance on advice that falls outside the limits of a virtual assessment.
  • Governing law: the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

3. Refund Policy

Please cancel as early as possible so another client can use your slot.

  • More than 3 days before the appointment: 100% refund.
  • Within 3 days before the appointment: 50% refund.
  • Within 24 hours of the appointment: no refund.
  • No-shows: no refund.

Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method.

4. Disclaimer

  • Not an emergency service. If you have sudden hearing loss, severe pain, facial weakness, head injury or severe dizziness, go to the nearest emergency unit or call your local emergency number immediately.
  • Virtual limits: we cannot examine inside your ear, perform hearing tests or remove ear wax online. Advice is based on what you tell us.
  • Referral may be required: we may recommend an in-person audiologist, GP or ENT specialist.
  • Information on this website is general and is not a substitute for personal medical care.

5. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Ear Clean uses only essential cookies and local settings needed for the site to work, such as your language greeting choice. If we add analytics or advertising cookies we will ask for your consent first. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work.

6. Consent for Telehealth / Virtual Consultation

By booking, you confirm that you understand and agree that:

  • Your consultation takes place by video or phone, using Zoom, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp or a phone call.
  • Virtual care has limits and may not replace an in-person examination.
  • Technology can fail. If the connection drops, we will try to reconnect or call you by phone.
  • Your information is protected as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • You may stop the consultation at any time.
  • If you are booking for a child or another person, you are their parent, guardian or authorised representative.
